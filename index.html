01

About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Biodiversity Economics at the German Centre for Integrative Biodiversity Research (iDiv) Halle–Jena–Leipzig, affiliated with Leipzig University, and a guest researcher at GEOMAR Helmholtz Centre for Ocean Research Kiel.

My background spans marine isotope geochemistry, biological oceanography and interdisciplinary climate research. I currently lead Work Package 7, Integrated Assessment, in the European SeaO2-CDR project.

02

Research

A

Marine silicon cycle

I investigate the marine silicon cycle, with a special interest in diatoms, radiolaria and silicoflagellates. I use stable silicon isotopes to understand nutrient cycling and develop tools for reconstructing past ocean conditions.

My work combines field observations, taxonomic studies and controlled experiments—from water-mass mixing in the eastern Pacific to isotope fractionation in siliceous plankton.

B

Marine carbon dioxide removal

I connect natural science and economics to evaluate marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR). My research focuses on how biogeochemical evidence can inform integrated assessment models, climate policy and responsible decision-making.

As leader of SeaO2-CDR Work Package 7, I coordinate research on how mCDR contributions can be represented and assessed in global models.

05

Field work

The ocean is both laboratory and archive.

My field research includes oceanographic expeditions and mesocosm experiments in the eastern tropical Pacific, with a particular focus on the Peruvian Upwelling. These campaigns connect water-column sampling, plankton communities and isotope measurements across changing environmental conditions.
