01

About me

I am a marine biogeochemist. My work spans ocean observations, experiments, isotope geochemistry and modeling.

My research has two main strands. I study the marine silicon cycle through diatoms, radiolaria and silicoflagellates, including experimental work and paleoproxy calibration. I also investigate marine carbon dioxide removal by combining biogeochemical and socio-economic perspectives. This work integrates observations into assessment models to explore ocean alkalinity enhancement in climate policy, including its risks, supply chains, transnational impacts and implications for the Sustainable Development Goals.

02

Research

Marine silicon cycle

I investigate the marine silicon cycle, with a special interest in diatoms, radiolaria and silicoflagellates. These silicifiers shape marine nutrient cycling and the biological carbon pump, while their remains preserve valuable records of past ocean conditions.

My work combines field observations, taxonomic studies and experiments, spanning the surface ocean to the sediment. I use silicon isotopes to study water-mass mixing, biological fractionation, recycling and burial, and to calibrate paleoproxies. I also investigate weathering and seafloor alteration, including the rapid marine silicate alteration associated with coastal El Niño.

Beyond silicon, I work on related nutrient and elemental cycles, including barium–silicon decoupling at the Congo River margin and nutrient availability in the Arctic Ocean.

Marine carbon dioxide removal

I connect natural science and economics to assess the potential, risks and wider consequences of marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR). I am especially interested in ocean alkalinity enhancement (OAE), including its interactions with marine life, fisheries and ecosystems.

As leader of SeaO2-CDR Work Package 7, I coordinate research on how mCDR contributions can be represented and assessed in global and regional models. This includes linking biogeochemical evidence with socio-economic scenarios, climate policy and responsible decision-making.

My collaborative work also addresses environmental impact assessment for OAE, future fisheries scenarios, the economic value of the ocean carbon sink at country level, and the role of blue carbon ecosystems in the wider CDR portfolio.
